# Rasoi — Social Media Branding Plan

**Goal:** Build a recognizable, trusted food brand on social media — not chase install numbers.  
**Product:** Rasoi — personalized Indian recipe app (pantry match, 1,200 recipes, 15 cuisines, partner checkout).  
**Audience:** Indian home cooks, busy parents, young professionals cooking at home.  
**Market:** India-first; Android-first product, but branding is platform-agnostic.  
**Date drafted:** June 2026

---

## 1. What “branding success” means (no install targets)

Measure **presence and recall**, not downloads.

| Metric | What it signals | Direction to aim for |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Profile visits / week** | People checking who Rasoi is | Steady week-on-week growth |
| **Follower growth** | Audience building | Quality over speed |
| **Saves + shares** | Content worth keeping / forwarding | Higher than likes alone |
| **Comment quality** | Real engagement (“tried this”, “Gujarati version?”) | Replies within 24h |
| **Brand search** | People search “Rasoi app recipes” on Google/IG | Appears over 60–90 days |
| **Saved Reels / Shorts** | Discovery algorithm fuel | Track top 5 monthly |
| **DMs / WhatsApp forwards** | Organic word-of-mouth | Screenshot and reshare (with permission) |
| **Consistent posting** | Brand discipline | Hit weekly cadence 4 weeks in a row |

**North star:** Someone sees a dinner Reel and thinks *“That’s Rasoi — the app that picks dinner from your pantry.”*

---

## 2. Brand foundation (set once, reuse everywhere)

### Positioning

**Category:** Your everyday Indian kitchen companion — not a celebrity chef channel, not a generic recipe dump.

**One-liner:**  
*Rasoi helps you decide what to cook tonight — based on what’s already in your kitchen.*

**Tagline options (pick one for bios):**
- *“Aaj kya banaye?”*
- *“Dinner, decided.*
- *“Cook with what you have.”*

### Voice & tone

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Warm, practical, like a helpful neighbour | Hype-y startup speak (“revolutionary”, “disrupting”) |
| Short Hindi-English mix where natural | Forced Hinglish on every line |
| Celebrate regional food (15 cuisines) | One-cuisine default (e.g. only North Indian) |
| Show real home cooking (messy counter OK) | Over-produced studio food only |
| Honest about shortcuts (frozen, tinned, partner delivery) | Pretend every meal is from scratch |

### Visual identity (social)

| Element | Guideline |
|---------|-----------|
| **Colors** | Warm kitchen tones — terracotta, cream, deep green (match app where possible) |
| **Type** | Bold headline on Reels; readable subtitles (Hindi + English if bilingual) |
| **Logo** | Small watermark bottom-right on video; full logo on static posts |
| **Food shots** | Real plates, thali, tiffin — not stock-only |
| **Screen recordings** | Show pantry match in 3–5 sec; don’t linger on UI |
| **Templates** | 3–4 Canva templates: Reel cover, quote card, “tonight’s pick”, cuisine spotlight |

### Bio template (all platforms)

```
Rasoi 🍲
Aaj kya banaye? Personalized Indian recipes from your pantry.
15 cuisines · Veg & non-veg · Partner grocery links
[Link when live: Play Store / Linktree]
```

## 3. Content pillars (rotate weekly)

Every post should fit **one** pillar. No random recipe spam.

| Pillar | % of content | Purpose | Example formats |
|--------|--------------|---------|-----------------|
| **1. Tonight’s dinner** | 35% | Core brand — decision + cook | “3 ingredients → dinner in 20 min”, pantry match screen record |
| **2. Regional roots** | 25% | Differentiation — 15 cuisines | “Gujarati comfort”, “Chettinad under 30 min”, festival specials |
| **3. Kitchen reality** | 20% | Relatability | “No coriander? Still works”, “hostel cooking”, “leftover upgrade” |
| **4. Rasoi tips** | 15% | Product education without hard sell | Meal planning, diet filters, partner checkout demo |
| **5. Community / culture** | 5% | Human brand | Polls, UGC reposts, “what’s in your fridge?” |

### Recurring series (name them — builds brand memory)

| Series name | Frequency | Hook |
|-------------|-----------|------|
| **#AajKyaBanaye** | 2×/week | Daily decision content |
| **#PantryToPlate** | 1×/week | Ingredient → full dish |
| **#RasoiRegional** | 1×/week | Rotate cuisines (15-week cycle) |
| **#FridgeCheck** | 1×/week | “You have X, Y, Z — here’s dinner” |
| **#KitchenTruth** | 2×/month | Relatable memes / carousels |
| **#FounderNote** | 1×/month | Builder story — authentic, not pitchy |

## 4. Platform strategy — where to show up

Focus on **3 primary** + **2 secondary**. Ignore the rest until primary cadence is stable.

### Primary (80% of effort)

| Platform | Brand role | Cadence | Best content |
|----------|------------|---------|--------------|
| **Instagram** | Main brand home | 4–5 posts/week | Reels (70%), carousels (20%), stories (daily) |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Discovery + SEO | 3 Shorts/week | Repurpose top IG Reels; add title keywords |
| **WhatsApp Status** | Personal trust layer | 2–3/week | Short clips, “tonight’s pick” — your contacts + close users |

### Secondary (20% of effort)

| Platform | Brand role | Cadence | Best content |
|----------|------------|---------|--------------|
| **Facebook Page** | Older demographic + groups | 2 posts/week | Cross-post Reels; longer captions OK |
| **ShareChat / Moj** | Regional (if Gujarati/Hindi is your lane) | 1–2/week | Same Reels with regional caption |

### Optional / low priority until Month 2

| Platform | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Twitter/X** | Founder + product updates only |
| **LinkedIn** | Monthly “building Rasoi” post — B2B/partner credibility |
| **Pinterest** | Recipe cards — long-tail SEO, low daily effort |
| **Telegram** | Cross-post to food channels (partnership, not owned brand) |

**Do not spread thin.** One strong Instagram + Shorts presence beats six dead accounts.

## 5. 30-day branding calendar (no install goals)

### Week 0 — Brand setup (before posting)

- [ ] Secure handles: `@rasoiapp` or `@rasoi.recipes` (consistent across IG, YT, FB)
- [ ] Profile photo + banner (logo + one hero dish)
- [ ] Write bio + pin one “intro” Reel (“What is Rasoi?” 20 sec)
- [ ] Create **4 Canva templates** (Reel thumb, carousel, quote, regional)

- [ ] Set up **Linktree** (IG bio): Play Store (when live) + best Reel + contact
- [ ] Define **3 hashtags** always: `#AajKyaBanaye` `#RasoiApp` `#IndianHomeCooking`

### Week 4 — Consistency proof (Days 22–30)

**Theme:** *“Rasoi is part of your routine.”*

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|

| **Festival / season** | Tie one post to monsoon snacks, weekend brunch, etc. |
| **#FounderNote** | 1 authentic post: why you built Rasoi |
| **Highlight reels** | IG Highlights: `Regional` `Pantry` `Tips` `Community` |
| **Review brand** | Which 3 posts got most saves? Double down in Month 2 |

**End of Month 1 checklist:**
- [ ] 20+ Reels/Shorts published
- [ ] 4 named series running
- [ ] IG Highlights organized
- [ ] Bio + visuals consistent
- [ ] Responded to 100% of comments for 2 weeks straight

## 6. Reel shot list (batch 10 in one session)

Record these once; release over 3–4 weeks.

1. **Intro** — “Open app → pantry → dinner in 10 sec” (face optional)
2. **3 ingredients** — aloo + onion + tomato → sabzi
3. **Regional** — one Gujarati, one South, one Punjabi (15 sec each)
4. **Partner moment** — “Missing dhaniya → Blinkit from recipe” (no hard sell)
5. **Diet filter** — “Veg · 20 min · Gujarati” selection
6. **Leftover** — “Rice + dal → new dish”
7. **Weeknight** — “Tuesday 8pm, nothing planned” hook
8. **Festival** — one seasonal dish from catalog
9. **Myth bust** — “You don’t need 20 spices for good dal”
10. **Carousel source** — “5 dinners from atta, rice, dal, aloo, onion”

**Production tips:**
- Vertical 9:16, 15–30 sec
- Hook in first 2 seconds (text on screen)
- Subtitles always (many watch muted)
- End card: logo + “Rasoi” — **no “download now” CTA every time** (branding ≠ ads)

## 7. Weekly operating rhythm (sustainable)

| When | Task | Time |
|------|------|------|
| **Sunday** | Plan 5 posts; write captions | 45 min |
| **Monday** | Batch 2 Reels (record + edit) | 90 min |
| **Wed / Fri** | Publish + Stories | 15 min/day |
| **Daily** | Reply comments + 5 min story | 10 min |
| **Monthly** | Review saves/shares; refresh templates | 30 min |

**Minimum viable brand:** 3 Reels/week + daily Stories + comment replies.  
**Ideal brand:** 5 Reels/week + Shorts cross-post + 2 carousels/month.

---

## 8. Engagement rules (brand, not growth hacks)

1. **Reply like a person** — “Try adding jeera if you have it” beats “Thanks! 🙏”
2. **Pin one helpful comment** per Reel (substitution, time-saving tip)
3. **Never spam groups** — add value first in Facebook/WhatsApp communities
4. **Credit regional food** — name the cuisine; avoid “Indian food” generic
5. **Reshare UGC** — builds “we’re a community” feel
6. **Avoid daily install CTAs** — mention app naturally 1 in 4 posts max
7. **Stay consistent** — same handle, colors, series names for 90 days before rebranding

---

## 9. Brand KPIs (track monthly, not daily)

Use a simple Google Sheet — **no install column required.**

| KPI | Month 1 baseline | Month 2 aim |
|-----|------------------|-------------|
| Posts published | 20+ | 25+ |
| Avg saves per Reel | Track baseline | +20% vs M1 |
| Profile visits (IG) | Track baseline | +15% MoM |
| Follower count | Track baseline | Steady growth |
| Comment reply rate | 80%+ | 95%+ |
| Top 3 content pillars by saves | Identify winners | Shift mix toward winners |
| Branded hashtag uses (`#AajKyaBanaye`) | 0 → first uses | 10+ external uses |

**Qualitative checks (monthly):**
- Does the feed look like **one brand** or random recipes?
- Would a stranger understand what Rasoi **does** in 10 sec?
- Are regional cuisines **represented** beyond your own background?

## 12. What to avoid (brand damage)

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Chasing viral trends unrelated to food | Confuses brand |
| Posting only app screenshots | Feels like ads, not a kitchen brand |
| Ignoring comments for days | Kills trust |
| Copy-paste captions across 10 groups | Spam reputation |
| Rebranding visuals every week | No memory |
| Comparing to competitors by name | Small brand looks petty |
| Promising installs / milestones publicly | Wrong goal for this plan |

---

## 13. Month 2+ — evolve the brand

Once Month 1 cadence is stable:

1. **Add one language** — full Gujarati or Hindi captions on regional series  
2. **Guest voice** — friend/family cooks one `#RasoiRegional` Reel  
3. **Seasonal calendar** — Diwali, monsoon, summer coolers from catalog  
4. **Pinterest boards** — export carousel slides for long-tail search  
5. **Newsletter (optional)** — “Sunday dinner plan” email; social drives signups  
6. **Partnership branding** — co-post with Blinkit/Zepto only if official; else keep generic “order missing items”

**Long-term brand vision:** Rasoi becomes the name people associate with *“what should I cook with what I have?”* — before they even open an app.
