# Rasoi User Guide

**Version:** 1.0.10  
**Platform:** Android (primary)  
**Website:** https://rasoiapplication.in

---

## Introduction

Rasoi helps Indian home cooks answer *"Aaj kya banaye?"* — what to cook today. Tell Rasoi what's in your kitchen, and it suggests recipes you can make right now. Save favourites, get meal ideas for breakfast through late night, and shop missing ingredients through partner apps.

---

## Getting Started

### Install the app

1. Download from Google Play, or install the APK from https://rasoiapplication.in/download
2. On Android, allow **Install unknown apps** if sideloading the APK
3. Open Rasoi

### First launch

1. You'll see the Rasoi splash screen while the app loads
2. If you're new, you'll land on the **Login** screen
3. Choose **Create account** to register, or **Explore as Guest** to try without signing up

---

## Creating an Account

1. Tap **Create account** on the Login screen
2. Enter your **name**, **phone number** (10-digit Indian mobile), and **email**
3. Tap continue to receive a **one-time password (OTP)** by email
4. Enter the OTP from your email
5. Read and accept the **Terms & Privacy** policy
6. Complete **Onboarding** (see below)
7. Set up your **Pantry** with ingredients you have at home
8. Optionally start a **free trial** of Rasoi Plus

> **Note:** Rasoi uses email OTP — there is no password to remember.

---

## Logging In

1. On the Login screen, enter your **email address**
2. Tap to receive an OTP
3. Enter the OTP from your email
4. You'll go straight to the app (onboarding only if you haven't finished it before)

---

## Using OTP

- Check your email inbox (and spam folder) for the code
- Enter all digits in the OTP field
- If email delivery fails, try again or use a different email address
- Disposable email addresses are not accepted

---

## Explore as Guest

1. On Login, tap **Explore as Guest**
2. Optionally enter a display name
3. Accept Terms & Privacy
4. Complete onboarding and pantry setup like a new user
5. To unlock full account features later, go to **Settings → Sign up**

---

## Onboarding

Onboarding has three steps for new users:

### Step 1 — Food preferences
- Choose your diet: Veg, Egg, Non-Veg, or Jain
- Pick your regional cuisine and favourite cuisines

### Step 2 — Notifications
- Allow notifications to get daily meal reminders
- You can skip and enable later in Settings

### Step 3 — Location
- Allow location to improve local recommendations
- You can skip and enable later

## Dashboard Overview (Home)

The **Home** tab is your daily kitchen dashboard:

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| Greeting | Personalized welcome |
| Tonight's picks | Recipes suggested for the current meal time |
| Seasonal rail | Curated recipes for the season |
| Recent cooks | Recipes you've cooked recently |
| Announcements | News from the Rasoi team (when available) |

Tap any recipe card to see full details.

---

## Managing Pantry

The **Pantry** tab lists ingredients organised by category (spices, vegetables, staples, etc.).

1. Tap ingredients you **have** at home — they highlight as selected
2. Tap again to remove
3. Your selections sync automatically when online
4. Recipe match percentages update across the app

**Tip:** After creating an account, you'll be guided to Pantry first so Rasoi knows what you can cook.

---

## Recipe Tab

Browse 4,000+ Indian recipes:

- **Search** by dish name or ingredient
- Use **quick filters**: under 20 min, leftovers, kids-friendly, festive, and more
- Toggle **pantry mode** to see what you can cook now
- Scroll to load more recipes

Tap a recipe to open it.

---

## Recipe Detail

On a recipe page you'll find:

- Photo, prep time, calories, difficulty
- Full ingredient list with pantry match indicators
- Step-by-step cooking instructions
- **Save** to bookmarks
- **Share** with family
- **Mark as cooked** when you finish
- **Shop missing items** via Blinkit, Zepto, or Instamart (Rasoi Plus)
- **Order food delivery** via Zomato or Swiggy (Rasoi Plus)

---

## Saved Recipes

The **Saved** tab shows all bookmarked recipes. Tap any card to open it again or shop for missing ingredients.

---

## Searching

1. Go to the **Recipe** tab
2. Type in the search bar (at least 2 characters)
3. Results update as you type
4. Combine search with filters for faster discovery

---

## Notifications

Rasoi can remind you about meal times:

- **Breakfast, lunch, snack, dinner, late night** — up to five daily reminders
- Tap a notification to open the suggested recipe
- Manage notification permission in Android Settings → Apps → Rasoi

Enable or disable notifications in **Settings → Edit preferences**.

## Subscription (Rasoi Plus)

### Free plan
- Full recipe access
- Ads shown in the app
- Limited partner shopping (2 shops per month after trial)

### Free trial (3 days)
- Unlocks grocery and delivery partner links
- Ads still shown during trial
- Available once per account when payments are enabled

### Rasoi Plus (Paid)
- **₹49/month** or **₹499/year**
- No ads
- Unlimited Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, Zomato, and Swiggy checkout

### How to subscribe
1. Open **Settings → Rasoi Plus**, or tap an upgrade prompt
2. Choose monthly or yearly plan
3. Complete purchase through Google Play
4. Use **Restore purchases** if reinstalling

### Promo codes
Enter a promo code on the paywall for trial extensions or yearly discounts.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---------|----------|
| OTP not received | Check spam; verify email; wait and retry |
| App shows offline banner | Check internet connection; cached recipes may still show |
| Partner shop button disabled | Upgrade to Rasoi Plus or use free monthly allowance |
| Trial ended | Subscribe to Rasoi Plus or continue on free plan |
| Pantry not saving | Ensure you're online |
| Push notifications missing | Enable in Android Settings; use release APK (not Expo Go) |
| Wrong account after login | Log out and use correct email |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a password?**  
No. Rasoi uses email OTP only.

**Can I share my pantry with family?**  
Not currently — each account has its own pantry.

**Is Rasoi free?**  
Yes, with ads. Rasoi Plus removes ads and unlocks partner shopping.

**Can I use Rasoi offline?**  
You can view cached recipes offline. Pantry changes need internet to sync.

**How do I delete my account?**  
Settings → Delete account. You'll confirm your email (registered users).

**Is my data exported?**  
Contact support — the app supports data export via API for registered users.

## Tips & Best Practices

1. **Keep your pantry updated** — even a few staples improve match accuracy
2. **Set your diet preference** — filters out unsuitable recipes
3. **Save recipes during the week** — plan weekend cooking from Saved tab
4. **Allow notifications** — never miss a meal inspiration
5. **Mark recipes as cooked** — improves your recent cooks and recommendations
6. **Try the trial** — test partner shopping before subscribing
