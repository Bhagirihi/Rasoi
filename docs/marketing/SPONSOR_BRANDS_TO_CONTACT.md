## 1. What you are selling

Rasoi offers **native sponsor slots** (not noisy banner clutter). Each placement is labeled **“Sponsored”** in the app.

| Placement | Where users see it | Best for |
|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| **Home** | Main tab after tonight’s pick | FMCG, paneer, staples, co-marketing |
| **Recipes** | Browse / search feed | Category brands, meal kits |
| **Pantry** | Restock / essentials screen | Dal, atta, oil, spices |
| **Saved** | Saved recipes tab | Repeat-meal brands |
| **Recipe detail** | In-recipe brand card on an ingredient | SKU-specific (e.g. Amul Paneer) |

Admin can configure sponsors in **Rasoi Admin → Sponsors**. Brands can also apply via the website form at `/#sponsor`.

## 2. Three types of “ads” on Rasoi

| Type | Who pays | Examples | Outreach |
|------|----------|----------|----------|
| **Native sponsor** | Fixed ₹/month (CPM or flat fee) | Amul, Tata Sampann, MDH | Email / LinkedIn to brand marketing |
| **Affiliate / performance** | % or ₹ per attributed order | Blinkit, Zepto, Zomato | BD / affiliate networks |
| **AdMob banners** | Google auto-fills | Generic ads | No outreach — already in app |

For **“show their ad on my platform”**, pitch **native sponsor** to FMCG and **partnership + affiliate** to quick commerce.

## 7. How much to charge (pricing guide)

What you charge depends on **MAU**, **placement**, and **brand size**. Use the tables below as **starting asks** — negotiate down for first deals, never go below your floor.

### 7A. By monthly active users (MAU)

| Your MAU | Single placement (Home *or* Pantry) | Home + Pantry bundle | Recipe brand card (5 recipes) | Notes |
|----------|--------------------------------------|----------------------|-------------------------------|--------|
| **0 – 1,000** (launch) | **₹3,000 – ₹8,000** / month | **₹6,000 – ₹12,000** | **₹2,000 – ₹5,000** | Offer **2-week free pilot** first, then paid |
| **1,000 – 5,000** | **₹8,000 – ₹15,000** | **₹15,000 – ₹25,000** | **₹5,000 – ₹10,000** | Gujarat/Mumbai regional brands often say yes here |
| **5,000 – 10,000** | **₹12,000 – ₹20,000** | **₹22,000 – ₹35,000** | **₹8,000 – ₹12,000** | Use CPM math (section 7B) to justify |
| **10,000 – 25,000** | **₹20,000 – ₹40,000** | **₹35,000 – ₹60,000** | **₹12,000 – ₹20,000** | Amul / Everest tier |
| **25,000 – 50,000** | **₹40,000 – ₹70,000** | **₹60,000 – ₹1,00,000** | **₹20,000 – ₹35,000** | Re-quote quarterly |
| **50,000+** | **₹60,000 – ₹1,00,000+** | **₹1,00,000 – ₹1,50,000+** | Custom | Add exclusivity premium |

**Where you are today (pre–Play Store scale):** start at the **0–1,000** row. First 1–2 sponsors: **free 2-week pilot → ₹5,000–10,000/month** is normal.

---

### 7B. CPM formula (use when MAU &gt; 5,000)

When a brand asks “why this price?”, show impressions:

```
Monthly impressions (one placement) ≈ MAU × 12 to 20
(Users open app ~3×/week; see Home or Pantry rail ~1× per visit)

Monthly fee ≈ (impressions ÷ 1,000) × CPM
```

| CPM (India food / FMCG app) | Use when |
|-----------------------------|----------|
| **₹40 – ₹60** | Early proof, local/regional brand |
| **₹80 – ₹100** | Default ask (5k–25k MAU) |
| **₹120 – ₹150** | Mumbai/Gujarat metro focus, exclusive category |

**Example @ 10,000 MAU, Home rail only:**

- Impressions ≈ 10,000 × 15 = **1,50,000** / month  
- @ ₹80 CPM → **₹12,000** / month (minimum ask)  
- @ ₹100 CPM → **₹15,000** / month (target ask)

## 9. Sales process (8 steps)

1. **Package** — PDF: 3 screenshots (Home rail, Pantry rail, Recipe brand card) + audience story.
2. **List 30 targets** — mix Tier 1 FMCG + 1 quick-commerce + 2 D2C.
3. **Send 10 outreaches/week** — email + LinkedIn.
4. **Offer 2-week free pilot** — track taps in Firebase Analytics.
5. **Share pilot report** — impressions, taps, optional checkout downstream.
6. **Convert to 3-month contract** — invoice + bank transfer.
7. **Deliver** — add sponsor in Admin → Sponsors (logo, copy, placement, affiliate search terms).
8. **Monthly renewal report** — upsell bundle or more recipe integrations.

## 11. Legal & disclosure (India)

- UI already shows **“Sponsored”** on sponsor rails and brand cards.
- Get **written logo / asset approval** before going live.
- Do **not** claim “official partner” in store listing unless contract allows.
- Follow **ASCI** digital ad guidelines.
- Issue **GST invoice** when required; consult CA when revenue grows.
- Keep signed IO or email approval for each brand.
