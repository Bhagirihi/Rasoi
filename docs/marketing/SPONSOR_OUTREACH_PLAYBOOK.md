## 2. How to contact them (step-by-step)

### Phase 0 — Prepare once (before any email)

| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| APK or Play Store link | They need to see the app |
| 3 screenshots | Home rail, Pantry rail, Recipe brand card |
| One-pager PDF | Logo, MAU (even if small — say “launch phase”), cities, 3 bullets |
| Privacy policy live | [rasoiapplication.in](https://rasoiapplication.in) |
| Pilot pricing decided | See [§4](#4-how-much-to-charge) — default: **2-week free pilot** |
| Sponsor form live | [rasoiapplication.in/#sponsor](https://rasoiapplication.in/#sponsor) |

---

### Phase 1 — Find the person (30 min per brand)

1. Open **LinkedIn** → People → search from [§1A–1E](#1-who-to-target-with-contact-details).
2. Pick someone who **posted in last 90 days** (active account = higher reply rate).
3. Prefer **Brand Manager / Digital** over CEO for FMCG; **Partnerships** for Blinkit/Zepto.
4. Save name + company in a spreadsheet: `Brand | Contact | Channel | Date sent | Reply`.

**If no LinkedIn match:** use corporate website contact form → write: *“Please forward to Brand Marketing / Partnerships — Rasoi app sponsorship inquiry.”*

---

### Phase 2 — First touch (same week)

| Channel | When to use | Rule |
|---------|-------------|------|
| **LinkedIn connection + note** | Always try first for FMCG | Keep note under 300 characters |
| **Email** | When you find work email or they accept connection | Use templates in [§3](#3-email--linkedin-message-formats) |
| **Follow-up** | Day 5 if no reply | One short bump — don’t spam |
| **Second follow-up** | Day 12 | Offer 15-min demo link (Calendly or “reply with time”) |

**Volume target (solo founder):** **10 outreaches per week** → 40/month → expect **2–4 serious replies** at early MAU.

---

### Phase 3 — Discovery call (15 minutes)

When they reply, book 15 min and cover:

1. **Show app live** — Home → missing ingredients → Blinkit tap.
2. **Show sponsor slot** — empty slot + mock “Amul” card (prepare screenshot).
3. **Offer pilot** — 2 weeks free, tap report at end.
4. **Ask their goal** — awareness vs trial vs pantry restock vs recipe association.
5. **Send recap email** within 24h — placement, pilot dates, assets you need from them.

---

### Phase 4 — Pilot → paid

| Week | You do |
|------|--------|
| 0 | Get logo approval + brand copy in writing |
| 1–2 | Live in app (Admin → Sponsors) · track taps |
| 2 | Send **pilot report** (§6.4) |
| 3 | Invoice for month 1 (or 3-month prepaid at 10% off) |

---

### Phase 5 — What channel for which brand type

| Brand type | Best channel | Avoid |
|------------|--------------|-------|
| FMCG (Amul, Everest) | LinkedIn → email | Cold call |
| Quick commerce | LinkedIn Partnerships + email | Asking for “ad budget” — pitch **performance / affiliate** |
| Local D2C | Instagram DM + LinkedIn founder | Long PDF decks |
| Affiliate-only (Zomato) | Network signup + parallel LinkedIn | Charging flat sponsor fee |

## 4. How much to charge

### Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Home rail** | Sponsored brand card on the Home tab (highest visibility) |
| **Pantry rail** | Sponsored card on the Pantry tab (restock intent) |
| **Recipe brand card** | “Use [Brand] paneer/masala” inside a specific recipe |
| **MAU** | Monthly Active Users — unique people who open the app once/month |

---

| Your MAU | Single slot (Home *or* Pantry) | Home + Pantry bundle | 5 recipe brand cards |
|----------|--------------------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **0 – 1,000** (you are here) | **₹3,000 – ₹8,000**/mo | **₹6,000 – ₹12,000**/mo | **₹2,000 – ₹5,000**/mo |
| **1,000 – 5,000** | **₹8,000 – ₹15,000**/mo | **₹15,000 – ₹25,000**/mo | **₹5,000 – ₹10,000**/mo |
| **5,000 – 10,000** | **₹12,000 – ₹20,000**/mo | **₹22,000 – ₹35,000**/mo | **₹8,000 – ₹12,000**/mo |
| **10,000 – 25,000** | **₹20,000 – ₹40,000**/mo | **₹35,000 – ₹60,000**/mo | **₹12,000 – ₹20,000**/mo |
| **25,000+** | **₹40,000 – ₹70,000+**/mo | **₹60,000 – ₹1,00,000+**/mo | Custom |

**Your default opener today:** **2-week free pilot → ₹8,000–12,000/month** (Home or Pantry) for 3 months.

---

### 4C. Add-ons

| Add-on | Extra |
|--------|-------|
| Category exclusivity (only Amul paneer, only Everest masala) | **+20–40%** |
| 3-month prepay | **10% discount** for brand |

---

### 4D. Floor prices (never go below)

| Situation | Minimum |
|-----------|---------|
| Any paid placement after pilot | **₹3,000/month** |
| Home rail with logo + tracking | **₹5,000/month** |
| 2+ screens bundle | **₹8,000/month** |

Below floor → **free pilot only**, no paid deal.

---

### 4E. What NOT to charge as sponsor fee

| Partner | Your revenue model |
|---------|-------------------|
| Blinkit / Zepto / Zomato | **Affiliate commission** (2–5% or ₹25–80 per order) — not flat ad fee |
| AdMob | Google pays you automatically |

---

### 4F. CPM math (when MAU &gt; 5,000)

Use when brand asks “why this price?”:

```
Monthly impressions ≈ MAU × 15
Monthly fee ≈ (impressions ÷ 1,000) × ₹80–100 CPM
```

**Example @ 10,000 MAU, Home rail:** 150,000 impressions → **₹12,000–15,000/month**.

## 5. How to convince them

### 5A. Your core pitch (30 seconds)

> “Rasoi is where Indians already decide **what to cook tonight**. We're not a recipe blog — users open the app at 6–7 PM, match their pantry, pick a dish, and order missing items on Blinkit or Zepto in one tap. **[Brand]** appears as a native **Sponsored** card at that exact moment — when they're planning food, not scrolling random feeds.”

---

### 5B. Why Rasoi vs Instagram / YouTube ads

| Their concern | Your answer |
|---------------|-------------|
| “We already do digital ads” | “Those are interrupt ads. Rasoi is **context** — user is already planning dinner and restocking pantry.” |
| “App is too small” | “That's why we start with a **free 2-week pilot** — you only pay if taps look good.” |
| “We need reach” | “You get **repeat impressions** — same user opens Home 3–4×/week during dinner planning.” |
| “Banner ads?” | “No clutter. One **native Sponsored card** per screen, ASCI-compliant label.” |
| “Can you prove ROI?” | “Pilot report: impressions, taps, optional downstream checkout taps to Blinkit/Zepto.” |

---

### 5C. Proof points to mention (honest at launch)

Use what is true today — do not inflate MAU.

| Proof | What to say |
|-------|-------------|
| Product depth | 1,200+ Indian recipes, 15 regional cuisines, pantry match |
| Intent moment | User opens app to answer “aaj kya banaye?” — high food intent |
| Checkout path | Missing ingredients → Blinkit/Zepto in one tap (show live demo) |
| Gujarat + Mumbai | Strong Gujarati & Maharashtrian catalog — name their cities |
| Low risk | 2-week free pilot, cancel anytime after pilot |
| Professional | Live website, privacy policy, admin-controlled sponsor slots |
| Disclosure | “Sponsored” label already built — ASCI-friendly |

**When MAU is low:** lead with **pilot + category fit**, not scale.  
*“We're in launch phase in Mumbai and Gujarat — perfect time for [Brand] to own the category before competitors.”*

---

### 5D. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “Send a deck” | Send 1-page PDF + 3 screenshots + APK link same day |
| “Need 100k users” | “Pilot is free — judge on **tap rate**, not MAU. Early sponsors get **locked pricing**.” |
| “Legal will take months” | “Pilot needs only **email approval + logo** — full contract can follow for paid phase.” |
| “We only do agencies” | “Happy to work via your media agency — we provide placement spec + reporting.” |
| “What's CPM?” | “At scale we price ~₹80–100 CPM; at launch we use **flat pilot fee** after free trial.” |
| “Exclusive with competitor?” | “We can offer **category exclusivity** for +25% — e.g. only your paneer brand.” |

---

### 5E. Regional angles that work

**Gujarat:**
> “Gujarati home cooks — dhokla, thepla, undhiyu — with Blinkit/Zepto in Ahmedabad & Surat. [Amul/Fortune/Gits] belongs on the Pantry and Home screen when they're planning the week.”

**Mumbai:**
> “Mumbai's 6 PM problem — pav bhaji, varan bhat, egg rice — with Zepto/Blinkit in 10 minutes. [Everest/Badshah] on the exact masala line inside pav bhaji recipe.”

---

### 5F. What makes them say yes (psychology)

1. **Low risk** — free pilot beats asking for ₹20k upfront.
2. **Category ownership** — “Be the only paneer / masala brand on Home for Gujarat users.”
3. **Founder access** — you configure their slot in 24h — fast vs agency bureaucracy.
4. **Co-marketing story** — they can post “Rasoi × [Brand]” on Instagram with screenshot.
5. **FOMO** — “We're talking to [competitor category] for the same slot.” (only if true)
